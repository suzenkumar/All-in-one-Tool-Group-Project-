Privacy Policy

Your academic work and personal data are protected with the highest standards of security. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the rights you have over your information.

Last Updated: August 7, 2026
Short version: We collect only what's needed to make our free student tools work. Your documents are never sold, never used to train AI models, and are auto-deleted after 30 days. You stay in full control of your data.

1. Introduction

All In One AI Student Tools ("we", "our", "us") is an AI-powered academic tools platform built for students. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and use our tools.

By accessing or using our platform, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our services.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information — When you register, we collect your chosen User ID, email address, full name, and a hashed (not plain-text) version of your password.

Usage Information — We collect data about how you interact with our tools, including the tools you use and your study progress (e.g. quiz scores, streaks).

Content You Provide — The text, documents, and notes you input into our tools (essays, math problems, notes, etc.) are processed solely to generate results for you.

Technical Information — We may collect browser type, device information, and basic analytics (via cookies) to improve our services.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain our AI tools and services.
  • To authenticate your account and keep your data secure.
  • To send you newsletters and notifications only if you subscribe.
  • To improve our platform, tools, and user experience.
  • To respond to your customer care requests.

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Storage & Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • AES-256 encryption for all stored data.
  • HTTPS encryption in transit on every page.
  • Zero-knowledge architecture — your academic documents are never used to train AI models.
  • GDPR-compliant data handling practices.

Your content is automatically deleted after 30 days unless you choose to save it.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

We use local storage in your browser to remember your login session, study progress, and preferences. These are stored on your device, not on our servers.

We may use basic cookies for analytics to understand how students use our tools and to improve performance. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work as intended.

6. Third-Party Services

Our tools may link to or integrate with third-party services (such as the AI models that power our generators). These services process the content you submit only to generate results and are bound by their own privacy policies.

We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of external websites you visit through links on our platform.

7. Children's Privacy

Our platform is intended for students and academic users. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights over your data:

  • Access — Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction — Update or fix inaccurate information.
  • Deletion — Request that we delete your account and data.
  • Restriction — Limit how we process your data.
  • Portability — Receive your data in a machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at groupprojectdrive0@gmail.com.
